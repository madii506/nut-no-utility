    06 — FAQ

    Questions.

    What is NUT?

    No Utility Token. A memecoin with zero utility and one job: its creator fees go to prostate cancer research.

    Does it do anything?

    No. No staking, no app, no roadmap. That's the point.

    Where do the creator fees go?

    To a public donation wallet, then to prostate cancer research charities that accept crypto. Every donation is posted on this page with its transaction.

    How do I check the donations?

    Open any donation's transaction on Solscan, or look up the donation wallet yourself. Everything is on-chain.

    Is NUT part of Movember or any charity?

    No. NUT is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any charity. It just sends them money.
